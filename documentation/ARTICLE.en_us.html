Solvik is a sans-serif family in seven weights, from Extra Light to Black, each with an italic. It is derived from Inter by Rasmus Andersson and moves it toward the geometric end of the grotesque genre: the round letters are widened until the o is close to a circle in every weight, the terminals are cut flat, and the italics keep the upright letter shapes for a, e and f.

The horizontal strokes are held closer to the verticals in the heavier weights, the figures are wider, and the spacing and kerning were rebuilt for the new shapes, with a minimum gap between any two Latin letters so that wide accents do not touch their neighbours.
